We have been hosted at Level Up, the monthly art series organized by Torino Creativa at Spazio Baom, in Turin at the Cortile del Maglio, for “Soglie di movimento.” For the occasion, we designed Specchi. <br>

Specchi is an interactive and generative installation created with TouchDesigner, inviting the viewer to explore the relationship between real movement and virtual movement. We set out to investigate the idea of space and movement in its most curious and spontaneous form. The digital mirror aims to create a moment of lightness and simplicity, where one can rediscover their bodily and spatial awareness.
